# Senara Social Media Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

- **Name:** Senara
- **Tagline:** "Cerita interaktif gratis yang mengajarkan life skills"
- **Voice:** Edukatif tapi nggak menggurui, ramah, relatable, Indonesian-first
- **Audience:** Remaja Indonesia 13-22 tahun, juga orang tua & guru

## Colors

| Use | Color | Hex |
|-----|-------|-----|
| Primary | Indigo | `#4F46E5` |
| Primary Light | Soft Indigo | `#818CF8` |
| CTA | Orange | `#F97316` |
| Dark BG | Slate Dark | `#0F172A` |
| Light BG | Snow | `#F8FAFC` |
| Text Light | Off White | `#F1F5F9` |
| Text Dark | Slate | `#0F172A` |
| Accent | Emerald | `#10B981` |

## Typography

| Element | Font | Weight |
|---------|------|--------|
| Headlines | Crimson Pro | 700 |
| Body | Atkinson Hyperlegible | 400 |
| Accent/Numbers | Crimson Pro | 700 italic |

## Visual Style

- Clean, minimal backgrounds (solid dark `#0F172A` or light `#F8FAFC`)
- Generous whitespace
- Indigo/Orange gradient for hero moments
- No clutter, no busy backgrounds
- Character silhouettes or abstract shapes OK
- NO stock photos of real people

## Logo Placement

- Top-left corner, 28px Crimson Pro bold, letterspaced
- Light bg: `#4F46E5`
- Dark bg: `#818CF8`

## Post Formats

| Format | Size | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| Square | 1080x1080 | Feed posts, carousels, quotes |
| Portrait | 1080x1350 | Feed posts (more screen real estate) |
| Story | 1080x1920 | IG Stories, Reels covers |

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | % | Description |
|--------|---|-------------|
| Educational | 40% | Carousels with life skills tips |
| Story Previews | 30% | VN screenshots with dialogue |
| Quotes | 20% | Powerful lines from stories |
| Behind-the-Scenes | 10% | How stories are made, milestones |
