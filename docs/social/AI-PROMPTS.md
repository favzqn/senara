## Story Preview Posts (VN Screenshots)

For story preview posts, use actual screenshots from the Monogatari VN player. No AI generation needed — the in-game art IS the content.

**How to capture:**
1. Open `senara.id/monogatari/?story=pahlawan-kesehatan-mental`
2. Play through to a visually interesting scene
3. Screenshot at 1080x1080
4. Add semi-transparent overlay at bottom with story title + CTA
