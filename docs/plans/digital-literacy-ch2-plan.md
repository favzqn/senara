# Digital Literacy Navigator — Chapter 2 Plan

## Chapter 2: The Deep End of Social Media

**Status:** Planning  
**Target:** 15-20 scenes  
**Duration:** ~15 minutes  
**Language:** Indonesian (with English/Japanese translations)

## Story Premise

Raka returns after Chapter 1's lessons on fake news and privacy. This chapter dives deeper into algorithm manipulation, echo chambers, digital footprints, and the psychology of social media addiction.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Understand how algorithms create filter bubbles and echo chambers
2. Recognize the signs of social media addiction
3. Learn about digital footprints and their long-term impact
4. Understand the psychology of likes, shares, and validation
5. Develop strategies for healthy social media habits
