# VN Specialist Review — Teman Baru di Kelas 8B

**Reviewer:** VN Specialist Agent  
**Date:** 2026-06-16  
**Story:** Teman Baru di Kelas 8B (8 chapters, 65 scenes)  
**Topic:** Deaf inclusion, BISINDO, friendship  
**Audience:** 10+ years old

## Overall Assessment: 7/10 — Good foundation, needs emotional depth

Strong heart. Genuine BISINDO integration. Inclusion message lands. But reads more like an educational script than a visual novel. Clannad Test fails — no urge to cry at climax. Potential is there, emotional beats need more space.

## What Works

- Scene 9 (Rafi approaches Alya) — best scene. Clannad-level character work.
- The buku catatan motif — Alya writing on paper is visual, tactile, real.
- Scene 21 (Alya reads whisper) — show-don't-teach at its best.
- Branching choice in Chapter 3 — three options that reveal character.
- Scene 27 (YouTube BISINDO) — emotional core, close to making people cry.
- Scene 54 (Mama/Papa crying) — right moment, needs more buildup.

### The Clannad Test

Would this make someone cry? Not yet. But close. Ingredients are there — Alya's isolation, Rafi's quiet courage, BISINDO connection, the performance. What's missing is SPACE for emotion to land. Add 3-4 quiet moments. Let characters sit with their feelings. Let silence speak.

Scene that SHOULD make people cry: Scene 54 (Mama and Papa watching). With the rewrite, it will.
