## Meeting Cadence

- **Weekly:** CTO + CPO sync (engineering + product alignment)
- **Bi-weekly:** Full board review (progress, blockers, priorities)
- **Quarterly:** Strategic planning (OKRs, roadmap, budget)

## Decision Framework

1. **Impact vs Effort** — Prioritize high-impact, low-effort wins
2. **User-first** — Every decision serves the learner
3. **Content depth > Breadth** — Complete stories before starting new ones
4. **Sustainability** — Nonprofit needs funding path for long-term survival
