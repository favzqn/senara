## Version 1: Google.org Impact Challenge

**Re: Funding Application for Senara — Free Interactive Life Skills Education for Indonesian Youth**

### The Problem

Indonesia is home to over 65 million young people, yet access to quality life skills education remains deeply unequal. Critical topics — mental health literacy, financial planning, digital safety, conflict resolution, environmental stewardship — are either absent from school curricula or delivered through outdated, passive formats that fail to engage a generation raised on interactive media. The gap between knowing something and truly understanding it is enormous. A textbook can tell a student what anxiety looks like; only an experience can teach them what it feels like to navigate it.

### Our Solution

Senara is a free, open-source, nonprofit platform that teaches life skills through interactive visual novel stories. Learners don't just read — they make choices, face consequences, and experience realistic scenarios. There is no sign-up, no payment, no app to install. Users visit senara.id, pick a story, and start learning.

We currently host **6 interactive stories** spanning **5 critical life skill categories**: mental health, financial literacy, digital literacy, communication and conflict resolution, and environmental awareness. Stories are available in **3 languages** (Indonesian, English, Japanese) and have accumulated over **1,250 plays** — organically, with zero marketing budget.

Our flagship story, *Mental Health Hero*, is a fully voice-acted, 5-chapter experience created in partnership with **diceritain.id**, where learners follow a character named Vani through anxiety and depression while practicing techniques like box breathing and progressive muscle relaxation. The story covers myth-busting, stigma reduction, and knowing when to seek professional help.

### Why It Works

Traditional educational content competes with entertainment — and loses. Senara reframes learning *as* entertainment. By embedding life skills inside compelling narratives with branching choices, we achieve what lectures and pamphlets cannot: emotional engagement, active decision-making, and memorable experiences. Users have told us "ini relate banget" — "this is so relatable" — which is exactly the point.

### The Ask

We are seeking funding to:

1. **Content creation** ($15,000) — Professional voice acting for 4 upcoming stories, illustration commissions for character art and scene backgrounds, and scriptwriting partnerships with domain experts.
2. **Infrastructure and tooling** ($3,000) — Domain, CDN, analytics, and accessibility auditing. Our static architecture means hosting costs are near zero even at scale.
3. **Outreach and pilot programs** ($7,000) — Partnerships with Indonesian schools, NGOs, and community organizations. Materials for teachers, classroom guides, and impact measurement.

Total ask: **$25,000**

### Scalability

Senara is built as a static website using Astro and the Monogatari visual novel engine. There is no server, no database, and no authentication system. This means our marginal cost per additional user is effectively zero — the platform can scale from thousands to millions of users without any increase in infrastructure cost. The service worker enables offline access, making it viable in low-connectivity areas across the Indonesian archipelago.

### Team and Partnerships

Senara was founded by a solo developer driven by the belief that meaningful educational experiences should never be paywalled. The project has grown through community contributions and a strategic partnership with diceritain.id for professional scriptwriting. We are actively building relationships with educational organizations to bring Senara into classrooms and community programs.

### Why Now

Indonesia's digital infrastructure is expanding rapidly — smartphone penetration is high, but quality educational content in Bahasa Indonesia remains scarce. Senara meets young people where they are: on their phones, in their language, with content that respects their intelligence and speaks to their real lives.

With your support, we can turn a promising prototype into a platform that reaches millions.

## Version 2: Kemendikbud (Indonesian Ministry of Education) Grant

**Re: Permohonan Pendukungan Program Senara — Platform Cerita Interaktif untuk Pendidikan Keterampilan Hidup**

*(English version below)*

### Alignment with National Education Goals

Senara directly supports the Ministry's vision of developing students who are not only academically capable but also equipped with essential life skills — the "Profil Pelajar Pancasila" competencies including critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and character. Our platform addresses five priority areas: **mental health and emotional resilience, financial literacy, digital citizenship, communication and conflict resolution, and environmental awareness.**

These are not supplementary topics. They are the competencies Indonesian students need to thrive in the 21st century, and Senara delivers them through a medium young people already love: interactive storytelling.

### What Senara Is

Senara is a free, nonprofit, open-source platform at [senara.id](https://senara.id) that uses visual novel-style interactive stories to teach life skills. Students make choices within realistic scenarios, experience consequences, and develop understanding through engagement rather than memorization.

Key features designed for Indonesian classrooms:
- **Bahasa Indonesia as the primary language**, with English and Japanese translations available
- **No installation required** — works in any browser on any device
- **No sign-up or account creation** — students can start immediately
- **Offline support** via service worker caching — functional in low-connectivity areas
- **Dark mode and accessibility features** — designed for diverse learning environments
- **Privacy-focused** — no personal data collection, no tracking of individual users

### Current Impact

Senara has already demonstrated proof of concept with meaningful engagement:

- **6 interactive stories** across 5 life skill categories
- **1,250+ plays** of our flagship story, *Mental Health Hero*, achieved organically with no marketing
- **Mental Health Hero** (5 chapters, voice-acted, created with diceritain.id) — covering anxiety recognition, stigma reduction, professional help-seeking, and relaxation techniques (box breathing, progressive muscle relaxation)
- **New Friend in Class 8B** (8 chapters) — teaching BISINDO sign language basics, inclusion, and empathy for Deaf peers
- **4 additional stories** in development: digital literacy, financial literacy, conflict resolution, and environmental awareness — each with concrete learning outcomes and interactive decision points

### Why Interactive Stories Work in Education

Research consistently shows that narrative-based and experiential learning increases retention and emotional engagement compared to passive formats. Interactive stories require students to *make decisions*, not just absorb information. When a learner chooses how to respond to a friend's mental health crisis in a story, they are rehearsing a real-life skill. This is the difference between knowing and understanding.

Students have responded to our content with comments like "ini relate banget" — the stories reflect their real lives, their real challenges, and their real language.

### The Request

We are seeking support for three areas:

1. **School pilot program** — Deployment of Senara in 10–20 Indonesian schools (SMP and SMA level) with structured lesson plans, pre/post assessments, and feedback collection. Includes printed teacher guides and classroom activity templates.
2. **Content development** — Professional scriptwriting with subject matter experts, voice acting in Bahasa Indonesia, and illustration for the remaining 4 stories. Each story targets specific Kompetensi Dasar and learning outcomes.
3. **Teacher training materials** — Development of a "Senara in the Classroom" toolkit: facilitation guides, discussion prompts, assessment rubrics, and alignment documentation mapping each story to relevant curriculum standards.

### Technical Sustainability

Senara's static architecture means the platform requires no server maintenance, no database administration, and no ongoing hosting costs beyond a CDN. It can be served from any web server, mirrored for redundancy, and cached for offline use. This makes it exceptionally sustainable for long-term educational deployment — the platform will continue to function even without ongoing funding.

### Commitment

Senara was built by an Indonesian developer who saw a gap between what young people need to learn and how that learning is delivered. The project is open-source (MIT licensed), community-driven, and committed to remaining free forever. With institutional support from the Ministry, we can accelerate content production, validate effectiveness through rigorous pilot programs, and make Senara a standard resource in Indonesian schools.

We believe every Indonesian student deserves access to quality life skills education. Senara makes that possible — one story at a time.
