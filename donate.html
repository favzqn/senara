Donation Transparency

We believe you deserve to know how your donations are used.

100% of Senara stories are free for everyone. Donations help us keep operating, not to create exclusive content.

?? Why Nonprofit?

Senara was founded on one belief: access to meaningful experiences should not be limited by financial ability.

We have no investors demanding profit. No revenue targets to hit. This frees us to focus on one thing: creating stories that truly make a difference.

The nonprofit model also means we don't need to sell user data or show intrusive ads. The reading experience stays clean and focused.

?? How Are Donations Used?

Every contribution is used to keep Senara running and growing. Here's a breakdown of our operational costs:

??

Illustrations & Visual Assets

Paying illustrators for characters, backgrounds, and visual assets for stories. This is our largest cost because good visuals make stories more engaging.

???

Hosting & Domain

Server and domain costs to keep the website online and accessible from anywhere.

??

Contributor Honorariums

A small appreciation for contributors who dedicate their time and expertise. Not mandatory, but we want to recognize their hard work.

??

Tools & Software

Subscriptions for collaboration, design, and platform development tools.

?? We have no physical office or permanent employees. The entire team works remotely and volunteer-based, keeping operational costs as low as possible.

?? Our Principles

??

No Exclusive Content

Donors don't get access to special stories. All content is available free for everyone.

??

No Paywall

No features locked behind payments. Senara remains completely free.

??

Voluntary Donations

No pressure to donate. If you can't, no worries. Just enjoy the stories.

??

Transparent

We are open about how funds are used. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
