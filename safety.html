Safety & Support

Guidelines for emotional wellbeing when using Senara.

Important to Know

Senara is not a replacement for professional mental health services. Our stories aim to increase literacy and awareness, not to diagnose, treat, or provide therapy. If you're experiencing mental health issues, please contact a professional.

If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services (911, 112, 999, etc.).

When to Seek Help

Reading stories about sensitive topics can sometimes trigger strong feelings. This is normal. But there are times when you need to speak with someone trained.

Seek professional help immediately if you:

  • Have thoughts of hurting yourself or others
  • Feel extremely overwhelmed and can't cope alone
  • Experience drastic changes in sleep, eating, or daily activities
  • Feel isolated with no one to talk to
  • Use alcohol or other substances to cope
  • Feel the story you read is too heavy and affects your daily life

There is nothing wrong with asking for help. It's a sign of strength, not weakness.

Tips for Reading Sensitive Stories

Some stories in Senara cover topics that may be heavy for some people. Here are tips to protect your emotional wellbeing:

  • You can stop anytime. If a story feels too heavy, there's no obligation to finish. You can pause, take a break, or stop entirely.
  • Pay attention to content warnings. Each story with sensitive topics has a warning at the beginning. Read it before continuing.
  • Prepare yourself before reading. Choose a comfortable time and place. Avoid reading when very tired or emotional.
  • Talk to someone. After reading a heavy story, sharing your feelings with a friend, family member, or someone you trust can help.
  • Try grounding. If you feel overwhelmed, try grounding: breathe deeply, notice 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear.
  • Reflect by writing. Journaling after reading can help process feelings and thoughts that arise.

For Parents & Educators

If you're a parent or educator who wants to use Senara for children or students:

  • Preview stories first. Read the story yourself before recommending it to ensure it's appropriate.
  • Supervise and discuss. For sensitive topics, accompany the child while reading and discuss together afterward.
  • Check age labels. Each story has an age recommendation (e.g., 13+). Use this as a guide.

You're Not Alone

Whatever you're going through, there are people who care and are ready to help. Don't hesitate to reach out.
